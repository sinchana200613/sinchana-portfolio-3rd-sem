A Little About Me

Hey! I'm Sinchana N — a 3rd-semester B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at REVA University, Bengaluru.

I'm at that exciting early stage where every new concept feels like unlocking a new level. Programming isn't just a subject for me — it's become a way of thinking. I write code, hit errors, get confused, figure things out, and then write more code. That's the loop.

I don't claim to know everything. I'm still learning — and that's exactly what makes this journey interesting. Step by step, I'm building my technical foundation, staying curious, and enjoying the process.
